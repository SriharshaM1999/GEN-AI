At dawn, a copper kite drifted above the quiet rooftops, carrying a note nobody remembered writing. Beneath it, the bakery clock chimed thirteen times, and a sleepy cat inspected the empty square. Mira followed a trail of blue buttons through the market, past jars of lemon peel and a violin playing by itself. At the fountain, she found a tiny brass key beside a map of an island shaped like a sleeping fox. The sea was nowhere nearby, yet the map smelled faintly of salt. She tucked both clues into her coat and heard a cheerful whistle from the old observatory. Somewhere beyond the clouds, a lantern blinked twice, then vanished.
